About the PPR Funding Scheme關於公共政策研究資助計劃

The Public Policy Research Funding Scheme (PPRFS) is a government-financed funding scheme dedicated to facilitating evidence-based public policy research by tertiary institutions and public policy research think tanks in Hong Kong. It is administered by the Chief Executive’s Policy Unit (CEPU), which supports the Government’s research and advocacy capabilities on long-term and strategic issues.

公共政策研究資助計劃（PPRFS）為政府資助計劃，旨在促進香港高等院校及公共政策研究智庫進行以證據為本的公共政策研究。該計劃由行政長官政策組管理，以支援政府就長遠及策略性議題進行研究及倡議的能力。

This research project is funded by the Public Policy Research Funding Scheme of The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (project in progress).

本研究項目獲香港特別行政區政府公共政策研究資助計劃資助（項目進行中）。

Any information, opinions, findings, conclusions or recommendations expressed on this website do not represent the views of The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region and/or the Assessment Panel.

本網站所載任何資料、意見、研究結果、結論或建議，並不代表香港特別行政區政府及／或評審委員會的觀點。
